===PARA 20 [Normal]===
ABSTRACT
===PARA 21 [Normal]===
One-camera football footage is a disorderly affair. Contact is often a late cut into the broadcast, post closure of players. We created a pipeline that can feed pose and minimal motion history into a short clip, analyze it and compute foul-like challenge scores against dive-like challenges, and that will gently push a simulated aerial camera to the challenged pair when it has an alert. This is our End Semester project of Computer Vision (23AID301).
===PARA 22 [Normal]===
The following is how we evaluate: CAS-FD clips are primarily evaluated after duplicate filtering is applied. This is a hold out split of 351:98:95 train:validation:test clips. Features stay causal. Past Frames are used to feed into 27 D pair vectors and 81 D scene vector. No tuning is needed for model and cutoffs. Leaving aside 2 results of a clip: CatBoost boosted-deep with a 1.0 s prefix and soft ensemble with the same cutoff. On the 95-clip test set the ensemble gets 64.2% accuracy, 64.6% balanced accuracy, 60.4% precision, 71.1% recall, F1 0.653, AP 0.650, ROC AUC 0.671. The complete list of comparisons is given in Table 4.2.
===PARA 23 [Normal]===
It was also taken for continuous scoring on World Cup Second Halves, it was tested out on Argentina vs France. Sample accuracy seems high as most frames are negative. Event F1 is about 0.061 and ROC AUC about 0.51. There's no way that's a soft song. Not sold as primary product! Additional checks include pose coverage on 'KTH Multiview Football II', and planned and survey camera framing. Five sample videos are provided and the desktop demo dumps the telemetry and replay, while a live ops dashboard is updated during analysis of the video.

INTRODUCTION
===PARA 99 [Normal]===
1.1 Problem background
===PARA 100 [Normal]===
Chases the story through broadcast edits changes. The shots are often wide and after the challenge has started are cut to a close-up. This is not ideal for warnings upon contact. Bodies collide into each other. Flood lights cause clashes on colours. Loss of ball/loss of direction of play by the referee. Typically, it is too late to make a good camera move by the time the receiver is in a "clean" tackle situation.
===PARA 101 [Normal]===
We divided the work into two tasks. On the short clips we attempt to use pose prefixes and motion prefixes to tell foul-line challenges from dive-line challenges. On Full halves we score uninterrupted and have a virtual "sky cam" when alarm goes on. With the long title, that lens concept is hinted at. Numbers we will uphold as pertinent for the course primarily originate from CAS-FD clip track.
===PARA 102 [Normal]===
CAS-FD provides short clips labelled. We hash & perceptual duplicate group so we kept 544 of 600 files processed. There are 95 clips (45 foul, 50 dive) in the test set held out. Only ordinary-play negatives will be included during training for the Quiet SportsD World Cup segments. To conduct continuous work, we have seven second halves of FIFA World Cup 2022 combined and matched to the moments when the ball was won from the opposite side to the stats on StatsBomb Foul Committed.
===PARA 103 [Normal]===
1.2 Motivation
===PARA 104 [Normal]===
Many Computer Vision assignments of this type only output detection scores. Live production is also interested in the direction in which the camera will look next. A threatened pair is the second early named and will remove a certain amount of body blockage on the replay path if a planned view. We never stop anticipating and still, the anticipation is weak. The clip track will be the one we can back as the splits and thresholds are frozen.
===PARA 105 [Normal]===
We wanted something that one could really run. Timing is not indicated on slides. The shipped demo provides broadcast side-by-side with a Pitch simulation, writes telemetry and updates the ops Dashboard while analysis is ongoing. The five sample videos are included in the repo and Launch Demo.cmd works without pulling the full sets once they have a fresh clone.
===PARA 106 [Normal]===

1.4 Scope and limitations
===PARA 114 [Normal]===
All clips and halves are only analysed offline as they are provided to us. Models are models that are frozen in joblib files. UI is a local demonstration of Tclp. No actual drone. If a calibration file is not present then pitch layout will be approximate. There aren't many continuous positives out there (47 out of 6,375 ARG-FRA frames) and there's no point in having an accuracy headline alone. Of the one matched continuous alert examined, contact was already in progress. Lead in time is not intrinsically anticipation.

===PARA 121 [Normal]===
2.1 Team Sports Pose Estimation
===PARA 122 [Normal]===
The pose(s) for the work were transferred from older part models into deep keypoints, suitable for fast speed of demonstration (includegraphics/fast.png). YOLO style pose heads were used since boxing and joints of multiple players are obtained from a single sweep. When we only analyse a few frames per second, MediaPipe Pose Landmarker can add more dense 3D landmarks within a box to aid the display and leg-motion estimation.
===PARA 123 [Normal]===
The difference between the close-up view and the view from a long distance are evident in KTH Multiview Football II. Close up makes up limbs that get lost in a wide. Sliding tackling is still painful due to overlap and blur. Our pose check had the same result: When we combined close up detections with wide, coverage increased, but tackle frames remained unclean.
===PARA 124 [Normal]===
2.2 recognition of action and foul
===PARA 125 [Normal]===
One of the most common types of foul paper format is the trimmed clips and clear labels. CAS-FD grades fouls versus dives, which is similar to what match review debates. When it comes to a whole clip its possible with big spatiotemporal nets but those are huge for a semester build, and they don't show which players mattered. We continued to use pose graphs and kinematics. Separation, closing speed and limb extension are more straightforward.
===PARA 126 [Normal]===
The clip track is based on tabular learners on cached pose features: boosted trees, histograms, and logistic members. Makes it easy to check thresholding / soft average against validation AP.
===PARA 127 [Normal]===
In this section, the agent learns how to anticipate and early predict the responses to the prosodic features.
===PARA 128 [Normal]===
There will be anticipation here, since it depends only on past evidence to make the scoring. On continuous halves the positive window is thin. If a teammate can beat us to a rebound between 0.75 and 2.00 s before StatsBomb Foul Committed, then we aren't getting many rebounds per minute created. Models that appear great on the balanced clips might not hold up the same manner during the ordinary play. Refractory gaps, rearm after the score falls out of range were needed in the alarm logic, otherwise the system chattered.
===PARA 129 [Normal]===
For validation halves, we selected the model and threshold utilizing $F1$ by the event, with the maximum number of false-alerts kept at one false-alert per minute. Then we froze that threshold, for ARG-FRA. Event F1 landed near 0.061 and AUC near 0.51. Those would seem to be other hard anticipation baselines. There's a long way to the clip-scores.
===PARA 130 [Normal]===
2.4 Planning sports shots with the camera
===PARA 131 [Normal]===
Automated cameramen will typically follow the ball/piece of group of players unless they get lost. Of the systems few are associated with fouls pre-cursors. A Skycam is planned with views that maintain a clear sightline. The typical costs are combinations of blockage, travel distance and projected view separation. Motion limits are used to prevent the camera from jumping from one optimum setting to another on each successive frame.
===PARA 132 [Normal]===
We grab camera samples of candidates in a radial direction around the point that we think is the pair, then order the samples by degree of obstruction and separation, then slowly increase the camera's velocity toward the selected sample. For the contact brackets that we examined, expected framing did not consistently outperform the approaches of survey or fixed view. It was uncommon for 'early alerts' to be given. We still retained the planner since the demo requires a visual relocate when the clip alarm is sounded.
===PARA 133 [Normal]===
2.5 Summary of gaps
===PARA 134 [Normal]===
There are many foul classifiers which do not use a clean causal prefix protocol on a named test split, with frozen cutoffs and a complete confusion table. Sample accuracy sometimes arises first when dealing with continuous papers that are imbalanced by class. Logged airplane course of flight is often ignored in camera papers. All the pieces are placed in a single repo: locked metrics, plots, models, and a demo class that students can take and do.

3.3 Perception and features
===PARA 163 [Normal]===
Detection is YOLO11n-pose. The tracker maintains the ids for the analysis cadence (currently 2 Hz, which is the cadence of clip feature caching). Regarding track clearing: A shot-cut reset clears tracks if the histogram jumps. Tracking close player detections automatically fills out to match wide track shots. We construct a 27-D vector from these six values for up to eight neighboring pairs. Those pairs are collected in an 81-D scene vector. Future frames, event names, label on files, and scoreboard time are not included in the feature vector.
===PARA 164 [Normal]===
If confidence is high enough, MediaPipe provides 3D landmarks for each box, relative to the hips. During alerts leg-motion magnitudes assist camera in focusing. In absence of 3D pose, the skycam revert to a plain stick figure.
===PARA 165 [Normal]===
3.4 Clip classification protocol
===PARA 166 [Normal]===
We decode clips at 2 Hz, while caching pose or pairs at 2 Hz. Prefix samples use cutoffs of 1.0, 1.5, or 2.0 s. Train split prefixes are used for training with multi time augmentations. Only the validation selects the model family, cutoff and threshold. The threshold is set to increase the poorest value of accuracy, balanced accuracy, F1, precision, recall in the validation. On top of boosted and soft-ensemble, we remain close to the optimum validation AP band. The 95 clip test set is scored one time.
===PARA 167 [Normal]===
Takes CatBoost boosted-deep for Step 1 with lock 1.0 s. The 1.0 s lock of Step 2 is the soft ensemble (CatBoost, tree and logistic probabilities). Caution: We don't tune on the test set.
===PARA 168 [Normal]===
3.5 Continuous forecasting protocol
===PARA 169 [Normal]===
The training is on 4 halves. Two validation halves decide on model and threshold based on event F1 with 1 false alert per minute as tie-break, AP. The best result is that of L2 logistic regression (C = 0.0005, threshold = 0.018414). There is only one test for ARG-FRA. If a score that is below the threshold is obtained, it will alarm at the rest of the times and maintains a 2-second refractory interval. State is reset when there is a time gap or when shots change.
===PARA 170 [Normal]===
3.6 Virtual camera control
===PARA 171 [Normal]===
When the alert is activated the camera make a number of samples at two altitudes around the mean future location of the pair. Each candidate pays when two targets get in line in the projected sightline, and receives a credit when the two move apart in sightline. Velocity 'drives' towards the lowest cost target whilst always obeying the limits of acceleration. No warning, camera scans back over a large area of the median cluster. Trails and destinations are sent to the map in the dashboard.

CONCLUSION
===PARA 296 [Normal]===
5.1 Summary of findings
===PARA 297 [Normal]===
The locked soft ensemble is around 64% accuracy, 65% F1, 71% recall, 60% precision, 65% AP and 67% ROC AUC at a prefix of 1.0 s on the held-out CAS-FD test set. Step 1 boost-deep is already done, to the extent of around 60%. Step 2 enhances the majority of the evaluation measures for classification. The intention of continuous foul anticipation on ARG-FRA is still close to chance based on the event metrics and is offered as unfinished work. Fusion corrections for pose improved with closets of the fusion. Camera gains were not uniform on the events that we viewed.
===PARA 298 [Normal]===

===PARA 299 [Normal]===
    Proposal of models for causal pose, selection of models and thresholds for foul versus dive clips.

5.3 Future work
===PARA 304 [Normal]===
However, there is a need for improved positive modelling of continuous models and an improved multi-view calibration. The camera policies that are subject to visibility can be worth trying. For example, use temporal transformers on pose tracks might increase the clip scores but only if we've got the compute – and we have to continue the validation-only selection. If one used a per-clip pitch homography then one would increase the skycam geometry. A real UAV should not be flown within this course deliverable.
